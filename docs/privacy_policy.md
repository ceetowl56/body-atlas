Body Atlas is an educational app with lessons and quizzes about the human body.

## Information we collect
Body Atlas does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email,
phone number, or location. We do not collect health information about you.

on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the app removes them.

and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and
information about how you interact with ads, to show ads and measure their
performance. Where required (for example in the EU/UK) we ask for your consent
before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice in the app
(Progress tab, "Ad privacy settings") when that option is shown.

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings
(Settings > Privacy > Ads). To learn how Google uses data, see
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
